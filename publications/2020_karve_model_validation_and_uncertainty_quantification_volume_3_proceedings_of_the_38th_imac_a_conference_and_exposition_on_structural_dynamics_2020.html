Fatigue Crack Growth Diagnosis and Prognosis for Damage-Adaptive Operation of Mechanical Systems

Pranav M Karve, Yulin Guo, Berkcan Kapusuzoglu, Sankaran Mahadevan, Mulugeta A Haile

Model Validation and Uncertainty Quantification, Volume 3: Proceedings of the 38th IMAC, A Conference and Exposition on Structural Dynamics · Proceedings

Summary

This paper describes diagnosis and prognosis of fatigue-crack growth for damage-adaptive operation of mechanical systems.

Research contribution

The work combines probabilistic Lamb-wave crack diagnosis with fatigue-crack prognosis to support operation planning under uncertainty.
