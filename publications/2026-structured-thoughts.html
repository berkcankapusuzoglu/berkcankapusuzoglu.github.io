Structured Thoughts for Improved Reasoning and Context Pruning

Zain Sarwar, Supriyo Chakraborty, Berkcan Kapusuzoglu, Chia-Hsuan Lee, Anirban Das, Stephen Rawls, Kartik Balasubramaniam, Sambit Sahu

arXiv · Preprint

Summary

Structured Thoughts organizes reasoning into alternating exploration and outcome blocks, and studies pruning exploratory text to reduce context use.

Research contribution

The work presents a structured reasoning format intended to retain conclusions while allowing intermediate exploratory text to be pruned.
