Multi-objective optimization under uncertainty of part quality in fused filament fabrication

Berkcan Kapusuzoglu, Paromita Nath, Matthew Sato, Sankaran Mahadevan, Paul Witherell

ASCE-ASME Journal of Risk and Uncertainty in Engineering Systems, Part B: Mechanical Engineering · Journal

Summary

This work develops data-driven multi-objective optimization under uncertainty for geometric accuracy and filament bond quality in fused filament fabrication.

Research contribution

The study uses Bayesian neural networks to model quality outcomes and compares robustness-based process-design formulations.
