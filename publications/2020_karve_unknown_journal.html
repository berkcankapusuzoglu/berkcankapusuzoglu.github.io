Resilience-enhancing operations of aerostructural systems under uncertainty: a digital twin approach

Pranav M Karve, Yulin G Guo, Berkcan Kapusuzoglu, Sankaran Mahadevan, Mulugeta A Haile

AIAA SciTech Forum · Proceedings

Summary

This work examines a digital-twin approach to resilience-enhancing operation planning for aerostructural systems under uncertainty.

Research contribution

The study connects health-state diagnosis, damage prognosis, and operational optimization to inform condition-based maintenance planning.
