Critique-Guided Distillation for Robust Reasoning via Refinement

Berkcan Kapusuzoglu, Supriyo Chakraborty, Zain Sarwar, Chia-Hsuan Lee, Sambit Sahu

International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2026) · Proceedings

Summary

Critique-Guided Distillation trains a student to refine flawed responses using teacher critiques as training-only supervision, without requiring critiques at inference.

Research contribution

The work introduces a training framework that separates critique consumption during fine-tuning from critique generation at inference.
