Multi-Level Bayesian Calibration of a Multi-Component Dynamic System Model

Berkcan Kapusuzoglu, Sankaran Mahadevan, Shunsaku Matsumoto, Yoshitomo Miyagi, Daigo Watanabe

Journal of Computing and Information Science in Engineering · Journal

Summary

This paper proposes multi-level Bayesian calibration for time-dependent multi-component systems using heterogeneous measurements.

Research contribution

The approach estimates component- and system-level parameters sequentially and compares offline and online calibration strategies.
