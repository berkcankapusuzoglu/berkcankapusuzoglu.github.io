Process Optimization Under Uncertainty for Improving the Bond Quality of Polymer Filaments in Fused Filament Fabrication

Berkcan Kapusuzoglu, Matthew Sato, Sankaran Mahadevan, Paul Witherell

Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering · Journal

Summary

This paper develops a framework to optimize fused-filament-fabrication parameters for polymer-filament bond quality while accounting for uncertainty in the underlying models.

Research contribution

The work combines transient heat-transfer and sintering-neck-growth models with uncertainty quantification in process-parameter optimization.
