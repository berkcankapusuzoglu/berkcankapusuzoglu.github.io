---
title: "Leadership"
description: "A practical approach to technical ownership, research direction, and dependable AI systems."
---

I approach technical leadership as the work of giving good research a clear path into dependable systems.

## Set a research direction

Start with a concrete problem, make the assumptions visible, and choose questions that can be answered with evidence. A clear direction helps teams connect exploratory research to a product or platform need without overstating what the results show.

## Own evaluation and release standards

Define what success means before comparing approaches. Evaluation should include the intended capability, likely failure cases, and the behavior a system must preserve. Release decisions should be grounded in those measurements and communicate remaining limitations plainly.

## Build through collaboration

Research, engineering, and product partners bring different constraints to a problem. Shared definitions, early technical reviews, and readable evidence help teams make decisions together and carry a method through implementation.

## Translate methods into reliable systems

The handoff from a paper to a system needs more than a promising result. It needs an implementation plan, repeatable evaluation, and clear ownership of what happens when assumptions fail.
